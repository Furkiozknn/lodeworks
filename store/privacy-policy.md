# Lodeworks — Privacy Policy

**Draft. Needs a real contact address and a public URL before submission.**

Last updated: 23 September 2026

## The short version

Lodeworks does not ask who you are. There is no account, no sign-in, no email
address, no cloud save and no advertising identifier collected by the game
itself. Your saved game is a file on your device and nowhere else.

## What the game stores on your device

- Your progress: the board, credits, research, Cores, depth and settings.
- A random identifier generated on first launch, used only to avoid
  double-counting the same install in anonymous statistics. It is not derived
  from anything about you or your device, and uninstalling deletes it.

## Anonymous gameplay statistics

If enabled, the game records thirteen kinds of event and no others. The list is
fixed in the code — anything not on it is discarded rather than sent:

first launch · session started · session ended, with its length · tutorial
completed · first production · first upgrade · stratum reached · shaft
collapsed · rewarded ad shown · rewarded ad completed · purchase started ·
purchase succeeded · purchase failed

Each event carries the random install identifier, the app version, a timestamp
and, where relevant, a depth or a count. No personal data, no contacts, no
location, no advertising ID, no device fingerprint, no free text. The set of
fields an event may carry is fixed in the code in the same way.

You can turn this off at any time in the game's settings menu. Turning it off
deletes the locally buffered events.

## Advertising

Lodeworks shows **rewarded video ads only**, and only when you choose to watch
one. Ads are served by Google AdMob.

AdMob is a third party and collects data under its own policies, which may
include your IP address, interactions with the app, diagnostics, and device or
advertising identifiers. Google's practices are described at
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland,
you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can
change or withdraw that choice at any time from the game's settings, under
**Ad privacy options**.

Buying the Foreman's Licence removes all ads and, with them, the ad SDK's
collection.

## Purchases

The single in-app purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Lodeworks never
sees your payment details. It stores only whether the purchase is owned.

## Children

Lodeworks is not directed at children under 13 and its Play target audience is
set to 13 and over.

## Your data, your device

Because nothing is stored on a server, there is nothing to request or delete
from us. Uninstalling the app removes your save, your settings and the random
install identifier.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
